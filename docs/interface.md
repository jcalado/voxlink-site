# Interface

Theme, accent colour, and what appears on the menu — all under **Settings > Interface**.

![Interface settings](/docs/interface-settings.webp)

## Theme

VoxLink is no longer dark-only.

| Option | Look |
|---|---|
| **Dark** | The usual dark VoxLink look |
| **Light** | Pale background, easier to read in daylight |
| **Follow device** | Matches your device's own dark mode setting |

![VoxLink in light theme](/docs/theme-light.webp)

**Follow device** is the one to pick if your phone already switches itself at sunset — VoxLink goes with it.

> Light theme is a good pairing with a windscreen mount in daylight. [Car Mode](/docs/car-mode) is a separate thing: it keeps its instrument-panel look whichever theme you're on, because a dark panel with amber legends is what stays readable behind glass.

## Accent colour

**Settings > Interface > Accent Color** tints buttons, highlights and active states. Pick **System** to follow your device's own accent (Android 12+ material colours), or choose from Red, Pink, Purple, Deep Purple, Indigo, Blue, Teal, Green, Lime, Yellow, Amber and Orange.

## Menu items

**Settings > Interface > Menu items** chooses which destinations appear on the full-screen menu used by [compact radios](/docs/hardware-controls#compact-radios).

**PTT** and **Settings** are always there — they're how you get back — so they're marked *Always* and can't be turned off. Everything else is yours to hide: if you never use APRS or Nodes, take them off the grid and give the tiles you do use more room.
